# Downdraft Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 13, 2026

Downdraft is a PDF reader. It is designed to read PDFs where you keep them,
without a Downdraft account, advertising, analytics, or a developer-operated
server.

## PDFs and Files

Downdraft opens PDFs that you choose in the Files picker or send to the app
using the system share sheet. It reads those PDFs in place and does not upload
their contents to us. The app retains the system permission needed to reopen
documents you leave open.

## Reading State

On your device, Downdraft stores the open-document list, current page,
bookmarks, reading preferences, and small thumbnail images used by the recent
documents widget. This information stays in the app's local storage and shared
widget container.

If you enable iCloud for Downdraft, the current page, bookmarks, and reading
preferences are synchronized between your instances of Downdraft through your
Apple Account's iCloud key-value storage. PDF files themselves are not copied
to this storage by Downdraft. We do not receive or have access to this state.

## Data We Do Not Collect

Downdraft does not collect personal information, create user profiles, use
analytics, track you across apps or websites, show advertising, or include a
third-party analytics SDK.

## Your Choices

You can close a document in Downdraft at any time. You can control whether
Downdraft uses iCloud in your Apple Account's iCloud settings. Removing the
app removes its device-local data; iCloud-synchronized state is managed by
Apple as part of your Apple Account.
